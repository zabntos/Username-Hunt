Local player database

This device keeps a small database of every registered player it has seen: you, friends from lobby score cards, and players loaded from the global board. It powers the leaderboard even offline.

Online leaderboard (Supabase)

Players who register a name appear on one worldwide leaderboard, and your stats upload automatically after every hunt. It needs an internet connection; the game keeps working offline.

How progress is stored

Progress is saved automatically in your browser's localStorage after every hunt. Different browsers, and sometimes different preview modes (for example an editor's preview versus Chrome), keep separate storage, so use Export and Import to move a save between them.
